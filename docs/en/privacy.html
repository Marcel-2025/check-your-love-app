2. Overview

  • No account, no own servers: The app works without registration. We do not operate any servers that store data from the app.
  • Names stay local: The names you enter, results, favourites and settings are stored only on your device. We do not receive them, and they are never sent to advertising or analytics services.
  • No analytics or tracking tools: The app contains no analytics, crash reporting or marketing tracking services.
  • Advertising: The free version is funded by Google AdMob. Google processes personal data for this (section 4) – according to your choice in the consent dialog.
  • Purchases: The optional Premium purchase is handled entirely by Google Play (section 6).

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (providing the features you use) or Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; access to device storage is strictly necessary for the feature you request (Section 25(2) no. 2 German TDDDG).

Retention & deletion: The data remains until you delete it – single entries or the entire history in the app, all data via “Clear app data” in Android settings, or by uninstalling.

Backups: Names, history and settings are excluded from automatic Android cloud backup. They may be included when you transfer data directly from an old to a new device.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

In the free version we show ads from Google AdMob: a banner on the test and history screens, occasionally a full-screen ad at a natural break (only after several tests and several minutes apart) and – only if you explicitly choose it – a rewarded video. With Premium, no ads are loaded.

Provider: Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland (for users in the EEA and Switzerland).

Data processed (by Google): in particular your device's advertising ID, IP address and approximate location derived from it, device and system information (e.g. model, Android version, language), information about the app, interactions with ads (e.g. impressions, clicks) and diagnostic data.

Purposes: serving and displaying ads, frequency capping, measurement and billing, fraud and abuse prevention and – only with your consent – personalised advertising.

What is not shared: Names and results entered in the app are never passed to Google AdMob. The app only requests ads rated up to “Teen”.

Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, Section 25(1) TDDDG) where information is stored on or read from your device or ads are personalised. Which ads are loaded depends on your choice in the consent dialog.

More information: Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses information from partner apps · Google ad settings. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings under “Privacy → Ads”.

5. Consent & withdrawal

Before ads are loaded, the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for your consent where required for your location. Your choice is stored locally on the device.

You can change or withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future: App → Settings → Privacy → “Ad privacy settings”. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing before the withdrawal. You can use the core features regardless of your choice.

6. Purchases (Google Play)

The optional Premium purchase is processed via Google Play billing. You provide payment details only to Google; we never receive them.

The app only receives purchase information from Google Play (e.g. product ID, purchase state, purchase token, order number and time) in order to unlock Premium, verify the purchase on the device, acknowledge it and restore it after reinstalling. This information is not stored on our own servers. Refunded or revoked purchases are taken into account at the next sync with Google Play.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (performance of a contract). Processing by Google is governed by the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

7. Sharing results

When you share a result, the app creates an image with both names and the result. It is stored briefly in the app's temporary storage and handed to the app you choose via the Android share sheet. The receiving app and its provider are responsible for any further processing. Please only share other people's names if they agree.

12. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. Ads are limited to content rated up to “Teen”.

13. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and the right to withdraw consent at any time (Art. 7(3)).

Right to object: Where we process data on the basis of legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), you may object at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation (Art. 21 GDPR).

As names and history are stored only on your device, you can view and delete them there yourself; we have no access to them. For data processed by Google, you can also contact Google directly. To exercise your rights, simply email flondy.dev@gmail.com.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the member state of your residence, place of work or place of the alleged infringement (Art. 77 GDPR).

14. Changes

We update this privacy policy when the app, the services used or the legal situation change. The version published on this page applies.
